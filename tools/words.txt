# ---------------- Parts of Speech ----------------
noun|noun|A word that names a person, place, thing, or idea|pangngalan|Salitang tumutukoy sa tao, lugar, bagay, o ideya|In the phrase the happy dog, the word dog is a noun.
verb|noun|A word that expresses an action or state|pandiwa|Salitang nagsasaad ng kilos o kalagayan|The word run is a verb because it shows an action.
adjective|noun|A word that describes a noun|pang-uri|Salitang naglalarawan sa pangngalan|In a red car, the word red is an adjective.
adverb|noun|A word that describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb|pang-abay|Salitang naglalarawan sa pandiwa, pang-uri, o kapwa pang-abay|In she runs quickly, the word quickly is an adverb.
pronoun|noun|A word used in place of a noun|panghalip|Salitang ginagamit kapalit ng pangngalan|Instead of repeating Maria, we use the pronoun she.
preposition|noun|A word showing the relation of a noun to another word|pang-ukol|Salitang nagpapakita ng kaugnayan ng pangngalan sa ibang salita|In the book is on the table, the word on is a preposition.
conjunction|noun|A word that joins words or clauses|pang-ugnay|Salitang nag-uugnay ng mga salita o sugnay|The word and joins two ideas in one sentence.
interjection|noun|A word that expresses sudden emotion|pandamdam|Salitang nagpapahayag ng biglaang damdamin|Wow is an interjection that shows surprise.
article|noun|A word that limits a noun, such as a, an, the|pantukoy|Salitang nagtatakda sa pangngalan, gaya ng a, an, the|The article the points to a specific noun.
determiner|noun|A word that introduces a noun|pantukoy|Salitang nagpapakilala sa pangngalan|In my book, the word my is a determiner.

# ---------------- Sentence Structure ----------------
sentence|noun|A group of words expressing a complete thought|pangungusap|Grupo ng mga salitang nagpapahayag ng buong diwa|Every sentence must end with a period or question mark.
phrase|noun|A small group of words without a subject and verb|parirala|Maliit na grupo ng mga salitang walang simuno at panaguri|In the morning is a phrase, not a full sentence.
clause|noun|A group of words with a subject and verb|sugnay|Grupo ng mga salitang may simuno at pandiwa|Because she smiled is a clause with a subject and verb.
subject|noun|The person or thing a sentence is about|simuno|Ang tao o bagay na pinag-uusapan sa pangungusap|In The cat sleeps, the subject is the cat.
predicate|noun|The part of a sentence that tells about the subject|panaguri|Bahagi ng pangungusap na nagsasabi tungkol sa simuno|In The cat sleeps, the predicate is sleeps.
object|noun|A noun acted upon by a verb|layon|Pangngalang tinatanggap ang kilos ng pandiwa|In She reads a book, the object is a book.

declarative|adjective|Making a statement|pasalaysay|Nagsasaad ng pahayag|I like coffee is a declarative sentence.
interrogative|adjective|Asking a question|patanong|Nagtatanong|Do you like coffee is an interrogative sentence.
imperative|adjective|Giving a command|pautos|Nag-uutos|Close the door is an imperative sentence.
exclamatory|adjective|Expressing strong emotion|padamdam|Nagpapahayag ng matinding damdamin|What a beautiful day is an exclamatory sentence.
simple sentence|noun|A sentence with one independent clause|payak na pangungusap|Pangungusap na may isang sugnay na makapag-iisa|The dog barked is a simple sentence.
compound sentence|noun|A sentence with two or more independent clauses|tambalang pangungusap|Pangungusap na may dalawa o higit pang sugnay na makapag-iisa|I wanted tea, but she made coffee.
complex sentence|noun|A sentence with an independent and dependent clause|hugnayang pangungusap|Pangungusap na may sugnay na makapag-iisa at di-makapag-iisa|Because it rained, we stayed home.
topic sentence|noun|The sentence stating the main idea of a paragraph|paksang pangungusap|Pangungusap na nagsasaad ng pangunahing ideya ng talata|The first sentence of a paragraph is often the topic sentence.

meronym|noun|A word naming a part of a whole|bahagi ng kabuuan|Salitang tumutukoy sa bahagi ng isang buo|Wheel is a meronym of car.
collocation|noun|Words that habitually go together|magkakasamang salita|Mga salitang laging pinagsasama|We say heavy rain, and that is a collocation.
connotation|noun|The feeling a word carries beyond its meaning|damdaming kaakibat|Damdaming nakakabit sa salita|Thrifty and stingy differ in connotation.
denotation|noun|The plain dictionary meaning of a word|tuwirang kahulugan|Ang literal na kahulugan sa diksyunaryo|The denotation of home is a place where one lives.
nuance|noun|A very small difference in meaning|maliit na pagkakaiba|Napakaliit na pagkakaiba sa kahulugan|Note the nuance between angry and irritated.
polysemy|noun|One word having several related meanings|maramihang kahulugan|Isang salitang may maraming kaugnay na kahulugan|The word head shows polysemy.
cognate|noun|A word related to one in another language|kaugnay na salita|Salitang may kaugnayan sa ibang wika|Nation and nacion are cognates.
false friend|noun|A foreign word that looks familiar but differs|mapanlinlang na salita|Salitang mukhang pamilyar ngunit ibang kahulugan|Actually and actualmente are false friends.
loanword|noun|A word borrowed from another language|hiram na salita|Salitang hinango sa ibang wika|Kindergarten is a loanword from German.
neologism|noun|A newly created word|bagong salita|Bagong likhang salita|Selfie was once a neologism.
archaism|noun|A word no longer in common use|lumang salita|Salitang hindi na ginagamit ngayon|Thou is an archaism.
euphemism|noun|A mild word used in place of a harsh one|malumanay na katawagan|Malambing na salita kapalit ng mabigat|Passed away is a euphemism for died.
dysphemism|noun|A harsh word used in place of a mild one|malupit na katawagan|Mabigat na salita kapalit ng malumanay|Calling a house a shack is a dysphemism.
idiomatic|adjective|Natural to a native speaker of a language|likas sa wika|Tunog likas sa katutubong nagsasalita|Her English is fluent and idiomatic.
